About.

Jonathan Paris is a Toronto music producer, mixing engineer and recording engineer who works across the full life of a song.

Technical detail matters. So does instinct. The goal is a record that feels clear, intentional and unmistakably yours.

In the room

Focused recording sessions that keep the energy moving and capture clean, usable takes.

Behind the mix

Balance, movement and impact without flattening the character out of the performance.

From the ground up

Custom beats and full productions shaped around the artist, not pulled from a template.
